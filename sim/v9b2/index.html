System Compensation Plan — How It Works
1 · PV — The Universal Currency
Every transaction generates Points Value (PV). When a member joins, their entry fee creates Package PV. When members buy repeat products, they create Product PV. All bonuses are calculated as a percentage of PV — then converted to pesos at the PV-Peso Rate. This creates a single unified accounting system where every flow is traceable to its PV source.
Bonus = % of PV × PV-Peso Rate
2 · Package Purchase Event Chain
When a new member joins: (a) Package PV is generated from their entry fee, (b) their Personal Sales accumulator increases, (c) PV flows up their sponsor chain increasing every ancestor's Group Sales, (d) their direct sponsor earns the Direct Referral % of package PV, (e) every upline L1-L10 earns their Unilevel % of package PV, (f) they are placed in the binary tree, (g) if their sponsor just reached 3 directs, Harvest fires, (h) Upline Support fires on every bonus paid. All eight steps happen synchronously in a single transaction.
1 Purchase → 8 Synchronous Calculations
3 · Repeat Product Purchase Event Chain
When a member buys a repeat product: (a) Product PV is generated, (b) their Personal Sales increases, (c) PV flows up sponsor chain increasing Group Sales, (d) every upline L1-L10 earns their Unilevel Repeat % of product PV (all-or-nothing personal PV gate), (e) member chooses Left or Right leg and product PV flows up the binary tree, (f) at each ancestor, left and right PV are compared and paired PV earns the Pairing %, (g) Upline Support fires on every bonus. Product PV flows up BOTH trees simultaneously — sponsor chain AND binary tree.
Binary Walk + Sponsor Walk · Same PV
4 · Binary Pairing from PV
Each member accumulates PV in their left and right legs (from both new member placements and repeat product purchases). When a member has PV on both sides, the lesser amount is paired and earns the Pairing % bonus. Excess unpaired PV carries forward (no flush — only the daily pair cap limits how many pair events per day). Inactive members are skipped in pair counting. Can be toggled off in Feature Settings.
Paired PV × Pairing % × PV-Peso Rate
5 · Income Cap & Reactivation
Cap = 3× entry fee. Applies to all income types combined (pairing, unilevel, direct referral, DFI, harvest, royalty, PV bonuses, upline support). All-or-nothing skip: if a bonus would exceed the cap, the entire bonus is skipped. Can be toggled off.
All-or-Nothing · 3× Entry Fee
6 · Daily Fixed Income
Fixed daily amount for active members, up to max days. Subject to income cap. Can be toggled off.
Fixed × Active Days
7 · Harvest Program (3×10 Matrix)
Requires 3+ direct referrals personally. Indirects need 3+ directs to qualify in stacks. Chronological stacking. Can be toggled off.
3-Direct Gate · 10 Levels
8 · Royalty Bonus — Leadership Ranks
Monthly evaluation based on Personal Sales PV and Group Sales PV accumulators (fed by both package purchases and repeat product purchases). Supervisor → Chairman. Can be toggled off.
4 Ranks · Group PV % + Repeat Net %
9 · Upline Support
Downline earns % when upline earns from any bonus (except DFI). Fires synchronously with each bonus payout. Can be toggled off.
Money Flows DOWN · L1-L10 %
PV Architecture: Package PV = Entry Fee × Package PV Rate. Product PV = configured per product. All bonuses = % of PV × PV-Peso Rate. Direct Referral, Unilevel, Binary Pairing, and Repeat Purchase bonuses all draw from the same PV pool. Personal/Group Sales accumulators feed Royalty qualification. Tip: Use Feature Toggles to enable/disable individual mechanisms.
